Gut21 Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gut21 ("the app", "we", "us"), developed by Asim Anil Kurdal, collects, uses and protects your information when you use the Gut21 mobile application on iOS and Android.

1. Information we collect

  • Account identifiers: an anonymous user ID created automatically via Firebase Authentication. If you choose to link Sign in with Apple, Google or email, we also store the email address and name provided by that service.
  • Health and wellness data you enter: symptoms, stool and digestion logs, stress, sleep, water intake, food notes and your 21-day program progress.
  • Activity data (optional, with your permission): step count and activity from Apple Health (HealthKit), Health Connect or the device motion sensor, used only to show your progress.
  • Photos and audio you submit: meal, menu and packaging photos, barcodes and voice notes you choose to analyze. They are sent securely to our servers for AI analysis.
  • Location (optional, with your permission): used only while you use the "nearby restaurants" feature to find suitable places around you.
  • Purchases: subscription and purchase status processed by Apple / Google and managed through RevenueCat. We never receive your card details.
  • Usage and device data: app interactions, crash and performance data, device language and a push-notification token (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Remote Config).
  • Advertising: free users may see ads served by Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers and approximate location to deliver and measure ads. We do not use HealthKit or any health data for advertising.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: AI meal, menu and voice analysis, symptom tracking, insights, trigger finding and doctor-ready reports.
  • To personalize your 21-day protocol and reminders (in your device language).
  • To process subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • To keep the service secure, fix bugs and improve the app.

3. Health data (HealthKit / Health Connect)

Data obtained from Apple Health or Health Connect is used only to display your activity progress inside Gut21. It is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, never shared with data brokers, and never disclosed to third parties except as required to provide the app's functionality or by law.

4. AI processing

Photos, text and audio you submit for analysis are processed by AI models hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS Bedrock). Your content is not used to train third-party models. AI results are informational only.

5. Service providers

We share data only with providers that process it on our behalf: Amazon Web Services (hosting, database, AI), Google Firebase (authentication, analytics, notifications, configuration), Google AdMob (ads for free users), Google Maps Platform (nearby restaurants), RevenueCat (purchase management), Apple and Google (app distribution and payments). We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. You may also email us to request deletion; we complete requests within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data from your device.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU, KVKK in Türkiye, CCPA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You can revoke camera, microphone, location and health permissions at any time in your device settings.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on access-controlled cloud infrastructure.

9. Children

Gut21 is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Medical disclaimer

Gut21 does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.

12. Contact
