Gut21 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Applies to the Gut21 app on Android and iOS

Gut21 ("the app") is developed by Anıl Kurdal ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how you can control it. Gut21 is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

2. Data from your device (optional)

  • Steps and sleep (Health Connect on Android, Apple Health on iOS): read only if you grant permission, used to relate activity and rest to your symptoms. Daily totals are stored with your history. We never use health data for advertising and never sell it.
  • Location: used only while you use the "restaurants near me" feature, to query Google Places. It is not stored.
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you take a photo or record a voice note.
  • Notifications: local reminders you enable in the app.

3. Third-party AI services and your permission

Gut21's AI features only work by sending some of your data to third-party AI services. Before the first AI request the app asks for your permission ("Allow Gut21 to share your data with Anthropic and OpenAI?"), naming what is sent, who receives it and why. If you choose "Don't Allow", nothing is sent and only the AI features are unavailable; the diary, water tracking, calendar, 21-day program and Trigger Finder keep working. You can see and change your choice at any time in Profile → AI data sharing.

4. Analytics and purchases

  • Analytics: Firebase / Google Analytics collects app-usage events (screens, feature use, crashes) with a device identifier. No health entries are sent to analytics.
  • No ads, no tracking: since version 2.9.31 Gut21 shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. The advertising ID is not used.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled by Google Play or the App Store and tracked by RevenueCat (purchase and entitlement status). We do not see your payment details.

5. Who we share data with

Only the service providers named above (Google / Firebase and Google Places, Amazon Web Services including Amazon Bedrock with Anthropic's Claude models, OpenAI, Open Food Facts, RevenueCat), each acting on our instructions to provide the app. We do not sell personal data and do not share health data with third parties for their own purposes.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your history is kept while your account exists.
  • Delete in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your account and all history from our backend and Firebase immediately.
  • Or e-mail gut21@kurdalapps.com from the address linked to your account; we delete within 30 days.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Google for up to 14 months.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, KVKK and others) you may access, correct, export or delete your data and object to processing. Use the in-app tools or e-mail us. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

Gut21 is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest in AWS. API requests are authenticated with your Firebase identity token. Access is limited to the developer.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Material changes are announced in the app.
