FAQ

How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gut21. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Gut21.

How do I restore purchases?
Open the Premium screen and tap "Restore Purchases".

How do I delete my account and data?
Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your logs and account.

Is Gut21 medical advice?
No. Gut21 is for informational and self-tracking purposes only. Consult a healthcare professional.
